FrameCap statically links libvpx (WebM Project), under its BSD-style license.
Source: https://chromium.googlesource.com/webm/libvpx/
The Windows build script copies the installed libvpx copyright and license
notices into dist/libvpx-LICENSE.txt. Distribute that file alongside the EXE.
The portable ZIP includes the notices for its bundled libvpx version.
No FFmpeg code or executable is included in the application.
